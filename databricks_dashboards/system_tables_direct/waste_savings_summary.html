Waste & savings, in brief

A two-minute read. Every query, step and column: the detailed guide.

What it is for

Shows what we pay for that does nothing, and what to change: idle SQL warehouses and cluster nodes, failed job runs and SQL statements, serving endpoints with no requests, and smaller flags without a clean $ figure. Auto-stop what-if shows what a shorter auto-stop would save and cost in cold starts.

How it works

  • A warehouse or cluster is billed while it runs. The checks put its start and stop events (warehouse_events) or its per-minute machine use (node_timeline) next to the work that ran (query.history, job runs), so the running time with no work shows up as idle.
  • Idle time, failed runs and statements, and endpoints with no requests get a $ from billing.usage x list price, and the five checks are stacked into one list of possible waste.
  • Auto-stop what-if replays the real gaps between queries at shorter settings: the saving, and the extra cold starts it would cost.

The query, simplified

The core of it; runs as is in a SQL editor. The dashboard's full queries, step by step: the detailed guide.

-- The $ of failed job runs in the last 30 days, per job, at list price (simplified: every DBU of a
-- run that ended failed).
WITH failed AS (
  SELECT DISTINCT workspace_id, job_id, run_id
  FROM system.lakeflow.job_run_timeline
  WHERE result_state IN ('FAILED', 'ERROR', 'TIMED_OUT') AND period_start_time >= current_date() - 30
)
SELECT u.usage_metadata.job_id AS job_id,
       ROUND(SUM(u.usage_quantity * p.pricing.default), 2) AS failed_usd
FROM system.billing.usage u
JOIN failed f
  ON f.workspace_id = u.workspace_id AND f.job_id = u.usage_metadata.job_id
 AND f.run_id = u.usage_metadata.job_run_id
JOIN system.billing.list_prices p
  ON p.sku_name = u.sku_name AND p.cloud = u.cloud AND p.usage_unit = u.usage_unit
 AND u.usage_start_time >= p.price_start_time
 AND (p.price_end_time IS NULL OR u.usage_start_time < p.price_end_time)
WHERE u.usage_date >= current_date() - 30
GROUP BY u.usage_metadata.job_id
ORDER BY failed_usd DESC;

The rules

TermMeans
Possible wasteCritical or Warn in one of the five priced checks, $ above 0, and at least $50 per 30 days (failed runs: no floor).
Warehouse idleRunning with no query; pauses of a minute or less don't count. Critical: 30+ idle minutes and 60%+ idle, or $200+; Warn: 30%+ idle, or $20+.
Auto-stop what-ifThe window's gaps replayed at 1, 2, 5 min (serverless) and 10 min (pro, classic); only settings shorter than today's.
Idle cluster nodesNode time near 0% CPU: 80%+ is Critical, 50%+ Warn.
Failed job runs$ of failed runs and failed repair attempts. Critical: 50%+ failed (3+ runs) or $200+; Warn: 10%+ or $20+.
Serving with no requestsTracked, billed and no requests: Critical; tracking off: Warn (turn it on).

Good to know

  • System tables are not real time: job and billing rows can arrive hours late, so today and yesterday read low.
  • $ is list price, not the invoice; idle $ is the idle share of the billed $.
  • Warehouse idle needs query history and warehouse events; a warehouse with neither reads Not assessed.
